# FLORES-200 devtest: our own unit lists vs the frozen p1536 (Task 5)

Made by `harness/flores_ours.py`, with the method of `flores_task5.py` (same FLORES files, sha256 checked). The unit lists are the ones in `design_units.md`, rebuilt by `design_units_unseen.py train` and written as standard `tokenizer.json` files (SuperBPE: the one-file form). FLORES reports on them; it does not choose one.

- **Word rule (all languages):** maximal runs of non-whitespace (str.split()); no word segmentation for Zh, Ja.
- **Encoding:** each of the 1,012 devtest sentences per language alone, `add_special_tokens=False`.
- **Mean:** the 11 Indic languages (Hi … Or).
- **Caveat:** our lists were trained on a public stand-in for sample v0 (FineWeb-2 Indic + Sanskrit, FineWeb-Edu English; no code) (100 MB or 1 GB), p1536 on corpus60 (#414), with 131,072 ids against our 100k or 200k.
