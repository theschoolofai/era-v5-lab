# ERA V5 Lab: week 1 update

**Date:** 31 Oct 2026
**Period:** 24 Oct to 31 Oct 2026

The ERA V5 lab of The School of AI started work this week. Every member read a sample of training text and labelled the problems in it, such as broken characters, leftover web menus and repeated lines.

## The teams

- **Data A:** Data A is conducting document-level dataset diagnostics, auditing data quality and normalisation issues, and building reproducible gold-data preparation workflows for downstream teams.
- **Data B:** Data B read Indian-language and code-adjacent text with native readers, labelled the problems in it, and began shared gold sets and cleaning rules.
- **Tokenizer:** The Tokenizer team rebuilt the baseline tokenizer, compared it with published references, reserved special-token slots, and trained a first improved candidate for Indian languages.
- **KE2:** KE2 built a byte table that gives each token its own embedding, and tested it on the lab's tokenizer.
- **Architecture:** Architecture wrote the week-1 config, the config checker, a tiny mixture-of-experts harness, and the experiment plan.
- **Performance:** Performance built correctness tests for mixture-of-experts layers, memory-saving training methods and attention, and a toolkit that profiles each part on the GPU.
- **Infrastructure:** Infrastructure developed Slurm-based job scheduling, GPU health diagnostics, and checkpoint recovery mechanisms to enable reliable training, resource management, and recovery from interruptions.
- **Monitoring:** Monitoring built a viewer and a tracker for the diagnosis work, and drafted a metrics logger, alarms and node health checks for training.
- **Evaluation:** Evaluation listed its benchmarks, built a harness for general, code and Indic tasks, and fingerprinted benchmark items to keep them out of training data.
- **Post-training:** The post-training team tunes the model after pre-training. They drafted a licence table and special tokens, wrote a chat format, and built a coding-agent test.
- **Release:** Release wrote the rules for public text, a style checker and a consent form for names.
- **Quantization:** Quantization (the team that makes the model smaller for serving) compared quantization tools and tested them on a public model.

## Benchmarks

Evaluation chose these benchmarks for testing the model:

- **Coding and agents:** SWE-bench Verified, SWE-bench Live, SWE-bench Pro, Terminal-Bench 2.0, tau2-bench, BFCL v3, WebArena, WorkArena, BrowseComp, OSWorld
- **Indic languages:** MILU, IndicGenBench
- **Reasoning:** GPQA Diamond, HLE (text-only)
- **General knowledge:** MMLU
- **Long context:** RULER, LongBench v2, ONERULER

## Next week

Next week, the data teams keep testing the cleaning fixes, Tokenizer retrains its tokenizer, Monitoring tests training alarms, and Release outlines the model card.
