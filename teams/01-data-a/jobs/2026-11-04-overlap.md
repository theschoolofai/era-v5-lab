## Definition of the requested matrix

For source A and source B, a cell is the number of distinct A document occurrences with at least one qualifying
H1/H2 match in B, divided by all A document occurrences. A document counts once per target source, irrespective
of how many matching copies it has. The diagonal applies the same rule within A and excludes matching a row to itself.
It measures documents participating in duplication, not how many would be removed after selecting survivors.

All train and heldout parts contribute to the population. Distinct occurrences use matrix rows and
`(pack, split, part, row_in_part)`; equal `doc_id` values across parts do not collapse them.
`overlap_matrix_counts.csv` preserves each numerator and denominator for review.

## H1, H2 and H3 scope

- H1: same SHA-256 of the existing normalized H1 key. H2 minimum lengths do not exclude exact copies.
- H2 prose: word 5-gram Jaccard ≥0.80, with at least 50 words on both sides.
- H2 math: word 5-gram Jaccard ≥0.90 and identical signed-number multisets, with no minimum length.
- H2 code: language-lexer token 5-gram Jaccard ≥0.85, after removing comments/licence headers, with at least
  20 nonblank code lines on both sides. Strings and preprocessor directives are preserved. An independent code-token
  pass catches copies whose raw word score falls below the diagnostic floor.
- Mixed-domain pairs: both domains' H2 rules must pass on both documents. Mixed code/text pairs use the code
  member's language lexer on both sides. Code/code uses each member's own lexer.
- Prose currently includes English, science, Indic and code-adjacent (`cadj-*`) text; math and the six `code-*`
  packs use their corresponding rules. The mapping is recorded in the summary.
- H3: same-language cross-source overlap is measured by cross-pack matches. Translations are not detected,
  consistent with the v1 design's stated limit pending TSAI confirmation. Indic thresholds still need calibration.

`overlap_matches.csv` records eligible matches once with H1/H2, within/cross-pack stage and word/code scores.
The raw pair CSV, histogram and document NPZ remain diagnostic measurements; their counts are not the final duplicate
rates. This job contributes overlap evidence to E2. Survivor selection, removal counts and the gold harm check remain
separate; these measurements do not authorize applying cross-pack dedup.
